Build a complete, functional mobile-first Japanese learning app called Japaneducation.

VERY IMPORTANT — LEARNING METHOD

This app must NOT be a memorization app.

I do NOT want to memorize Japanese words, Bengali meanings, or grammar rules.

I want to learn Japanese naturally, similar to how a Japanese child learns their first language.

The learning process must be:

HEAR → UNDERSTAND → COPY → USE → REPEAT → NATURALLY REMEMBER

The learner should gradually understand Japanese directly, without translating every sentence into Bengali.

Focus ONLY on JLPT N5 Japanese.

---

1. START WITH LISTENING

Introduce Japanese through short everyday situations.

Example:

A person wakes up.

Audio:

「おはよう。」

Show the situation first.

Then reveal the meaning:

“Good morning / সুপ্রভাত”

Do not ask the learner to memorize the word.

Let them hear it repeatedly in different situations.

---

2. LEARN THROUGH REAL SITUATIONS

Create N5 situations such as:

- Morning
- Home
- Family
- School
- Meeting someone
- Eating
- Drinking
- Shopping
- Going somewhere
- Coming home
- Asking simple questions
- Daily activities

Each lesson should feel like a small real-life experience.

---

3. LEARN SENTENCES, NOT WORD LISTS

Do NOT present large vocabulary lists.

Instead teach naturally through sentences.

Example:

「これは ほんです。」

Then:

「これは みずです。」

Then:

「これは ねこです。」

The learner should gradually notice the pattern.

Only after several examples give a very short explanation.

---

4. NO GRAMMAR MEMORIZATION

Do not begin lessons with grammar rules.

Use:

Examples → Repetition → Pattern recognition → Simple explanation → Practice

For example, allow the learner to experience:

「わたしは がくせいです。」

「わたしは バングラデシュじんです。」

Then briefly explain what 「は」 and 「です」 are doing.

---

5. LISTENING FIRST

Every lesson should begin with Japanese audio whenever possible.

Use browser Japanese speech synthesis.

Provide:

🔊 Listen
🔁 Listen again
🗣️ Repeat

The learner should hear Japanese many times before being asked to remember anything.

---

6. SPEAKING LIKE A CHILD

After hearing a sentence, let the learner repeat it.

Do not punish small mistakes.

If browser speech recognition is available, make it optional.

If unavailable, the lesson must still work.

The goal is confidence and natural repetition.

---

7. HIRAGANA AND KATAKANA

Teach Hiragana and Katakana gradually.

Do not make the learner memorize a giant chart first.

Introduce characters through words they already encountered.

Example:

「ねこ」

Then teach:

ね + こ

Then show how the sounds combine.

Do the same with Katakana.

---

8. KANJI

Introduce only useful N5 Kanji gradually.

Do not give a large Kanji memorization list.

Example:

みず → 水

Then show:

水を のみます。

Let the learner see the same Kanji repeatedly in meaningful sentences.

---

9. CONVERSATION

Create very short interactive conversations.

Example:

A: 「こんにちは。」

B: 「こんにちは。」

A: 「おなまえは？」

B: 「インジャムです。」

The learner should listen first, then choose/speak the response.

Gradually increase the conversation length.

---

10. UNDERSTANDING WITHOUT TRANSLATION

At the beginning, Bengali explanations are allowed.

But do not show Bengali translation immediately for everything.

Use:

Japanese audio → situation → learner guesses meaning → reveal meaning

As the learner improves, reduce Bengali support.

The goal is eventually:

Japanese → Meaning

instead of:

Japanese → Bengali → Meaning

---

11. N5 ONLY

Do not add N4, N3, N2 or N1.

Only build a complete beginner/N5 learning journey.

Cover essential N5:

- greetings
- introductions
- family
- numbers
- time
- dates
- food
- home
- school
- shopping
- daily activities
- basic questions
- basic answers
- essential N5 grammar
- Hiragana
- Katakana
- essential N5 Kanji
- listening
- speaking
- reading
- writing
- simple conversations

---

12. DAILY LEARNING

Each day should feel like learning a little Japanese naturally.

Example:

Listen → Understand → Repeat → Use → Review

Do not make the learner memorize 20 or 50 words per day.

Reintroduce previously learned Japanese naturally inside new situations.

---

13. REVIEW

Do NOT make review only:

“What is the Bengali meaning of this word?”

Instead use:

- Listen and understand
- Choose the correct situation
- Complete a sentence
- Respond to a conversation
- Repeat a sentence
- Recognize Hiragana/Katakana
- Recognize Kanji
- Understand simple Japanese

---

14. APP DESIGN

Create a modern, friendly, simple mobile interface.

Main sections:

- Home
- Learn
- Daily Lesson
- Listening
- Speaking
- Conversation
- Hiragana
- Katakana
- Kanji
- Review
- Progress
- Settings

All buttons and pages must actually work.

Use localStorage/IndexedDB for progress.

Make it mobile-friendly and compatible with Android WebView/Kodular.

No paid APIs.

Do not create only a visual prototype.

Build a genuinely functional N5 Japanese learning app.

FINAL PRINCIPLE

The learner should feel:

“I am experiencing and using Japanese.”

NOT:

“I am memorizing Japanese words and grammar.”

Build the entire N5 learning experience around this principle.
